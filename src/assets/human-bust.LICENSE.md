# Human bust mesh

`human-bust.json` is derived from the [MakeHuman Community base mesh](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj), a [CC0 1.0 asset](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/LICENSE.md#c-the-license-for-the-bundled-assets).

The mesh keeps the head and neckline from the body group and the two eye helpers. The head has been recentered, triangulated, and softly shaped around the mouth. Positions are signed integers at 1/4096 world units. The eye vertices begin at `eyeStart`, and `indices` encodes the triangle list.
